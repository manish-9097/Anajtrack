🧾 Information Available to Officer

Dashboard Overview

👨‍🌾 Farmer Information

Total registered farmers, farmer ID, registration status, selected crop, booked slot, procurement centre and entry status.

🌾 Crop Information

Crop type, number of farmers per crop, total quantity in quintals and crop-wise procurement contribution.

📍 Location Information

State, district, block and Gram Panchayat level procurement statistics for monitoring and administration.

🏪 Centre Information

Procurement centre-wise farmer entries, active slots, queue information and quantity received.

⚖️ Quantity Information

Total procurement quantity, crop-wise quantity and location-wise quantity received from farmers.

📅 Slot Information

Daily farmer entries, booked slots, token numbers and procurement activity for selected locations.
