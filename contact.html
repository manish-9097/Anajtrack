Frequently Asked Questions

How can I register as a farmer? +
Go to the Farmer Registration option from the home page and enter the required details.
Where can I check my token? +
After farmer login, the assigned token and scheduled slot can be displayed in the farmer dashboard.
How can I find my procurement centre? +
Use the Procurement Centres page and select the relevant location to find available procurement centre information.
I am facing a technical problem. What should I do? +
You can submit your issue using the contact form above with your name, mobile number and a description of the problem.
