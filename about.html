About AnajTrack

Connecting Farmers with a Smarter Grain Procurement System

Who We Are

🌾 What is AnajTrack?

AnajTrack is a digital platform designed to make agricultural grain procurement more organized, transparent and convenient for farmers.

Through AnajTrack, farmers can register themselves, provide crop details, select the required procurement centre and receive a token or slot for bringing their produce to the centre.

The system connects farmers, PACS procurement centres and government officials through a single digital platform.

What AnajTrack Provides

👨‍🌾

Farmer Registration

Farmers can register their details and provide information about the crop they want to sell.

🎟️

Token System

Farmers can receive a token and scheduled slot for bringing their produce to the procurement centre.

🏢

PACS Connection

Procurement centres can manage farmers, tokens, verification and procurement records.

🌾

Crop Information

Crop type, quantity and other procurement-related information can be maintained digitally.

📊

Digital Records

Procurement information can be organized for easier monitoring and reporting.

🔔

Better Communication

Important token, slot and procurement information can be communicated through the platform.

Our Mission

AnajTrack aims to provide a simple digital system that helps organize the grain procurement journey from farmer registration to procurement. The goal is to reduce unnecessary waiting, improve record management and make the process easier for farmers and procurement centres.
